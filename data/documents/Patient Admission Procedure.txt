# Patient Admission Procedure

**Department:** General Administration  
**Document Type:** Hospital Standard Operating Procedure  
**Version:** 1.0

## Purpose
To describe the administrative workflow for admitting patients into the hospital.

## Admission Workflow

1. Register the patient's basic details.
2. Verify patient identification information.
3. Record the admission request.
4. Assign the appropriate department.
5. Check bed availability.
6. Allocate an available bed.
7. Record the assigned doctor.
8. Confirm admission details in the hospital system.

## Documentation
Maintain accurate patient registration and admission records.

## Review
The hospital administration team should periodically review this procedure.
